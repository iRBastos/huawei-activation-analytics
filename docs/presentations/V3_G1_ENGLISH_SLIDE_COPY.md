# V3 — G1 English Slide Copy / Product Capabilities Walkthrough

**Version:** First complete copy-locked candidate for layout (15 slides, English).  
**Purpose:** The reader should know what they can do in the real tool, where to click, what the result means and which commercial action it can support.  
**Format:** One main message, one UI proof, one application/decision per slide. This is on-slide wording, not a presenter script. All example values are synthetic.

## 01 — From Activation Data to Channel Intelligence
**Main statement:** Turn monthly activation records into evidence-led decisions about products, installer activity and channel priorities.

**YOU CAN:** Explore equipment performance, investigate individual partners, compare activity and decide which changes deserve attention.

**Software evidence:** Overview screenshot (S01), with an editable navigation map of the seven modules: Overview, Inverters, Batteries & BMS, Comparisons, Targets, Installers and Data.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Better visibility of what changed, where to investigate and how to support the installer channel.

**Qualification:** Offline application · Data refreshed manually · Illustrative synthetic data.

## 02 — Import, Validate and Trust the Source
**Main statement:** Bring equipment and installer reports together in one controlled analysis workspace.

**YOU CAN:** Import monthly XLSX/CSV matrices separately, review dates and values, verify source totals and confirm changes before saving.

**Software evidence:** Authentic equipment and installer import-validation previews (S02/S03). Never show S03's contradictory “No file chosen” row.

**PROCESS:** Export source files → Review the mapping → Validate coverage and totals → Confirm the import.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Avoid mistaken zero activity, duplicate counts and decisions based on incomplete reports.

**Qualification:** Missing month or omitted account in a partial installer import is unknown, not automatically zero.

## 03 — See the Portfolio at a Glance
**Main statement:** Start with one consolidated view of recorded activity and its recent movement.

**YOU CAN:** Select reporting months; filter category, family or model; review inverter, BMS, battery-module and activated-capacity KPIs.

**Software evidence:** Overview dashboard with filters (S01/S04).

**EXAMPLE:** 6,267 inverter and 4,602 BMS activations, January–September 2026.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Establish the baseline for monthly product and channel discussions.

**Qualification:** Recorded activations, not sales or verified installed systems.

## 04 — Understand Inverter Models and Movement
**Main statement:** Know which inverter models are contributing to reported volume — and which are changing.

**YOU CAN:** Rank models by activations, examine signed contributions to change, select individual models for historical trend charts and inspect phase mix.

**Software evidence:** N01 inverter volume ranking and N02b per-model trend, with optional N02 model picker for source interaction.

**EXAMPLE:** In the fictional January–September 2026 data, SUN2000-5K-LB0 recorded 1,533 activations.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Focus product training, stock conversations and distributor feedback on the models showing meaningful movement.

**Qualification:** Models present in the catalogue do not necessarily have recorded activations, and no account-to-model join is available.

## 05 — Analyse Batteries, BMS and Capacity
**Main statement:** See storage-related activity beyond the inverter totals.

**YOU CAN:** Compare S0/S1 module families, number of modules, activated kWh capacity, BMS activations and module/BMS product mix.

**Software evidence:** N03 family capacity and BMS ratios, N04 battery module mix (N04b alternative for BMS mix).

**EXAMPLE:** January–September 2026: S0 recorded 12,010 kWh and S1 44,345 kWh in the fictional dataset.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Investigate shifts in recorded storage-product mix and associated partner questions.

**Qualification:** Capacity is derived from recorded module quantities × catalogue kWh. BMS/inverter or module/BMS activation ratios do not establish physical battery pairing.

## 06 — Compare Periods with Evidence
**Main statement:** Quantify what moved, using transparent comparison windows.

**YOU CAN:** Choose a product metric, compare current and previous complete months, check the absolute and percentage difference and read month-level evidence.

**Software evidence:** N05 period totals and N05b comparative monthly profile.

**EXAMPLE:** Compare July–September with April–June 2026 using three months on each side.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Identify changes worth discussing without letting incomplete periods or different durations distort the message.

**Qualification:** A comparison with uneven or missing coverage requires caution; do not manufacture a percentage from a zero baseline.

## 07 — Set and Track Activation Targets
**Main statement:** See actual recorded activity against an explicit commercial objective.

**YOU CAN:** Enter monthly targets by category or model; inspect Actual, Target, Attainment and Gap across a selected period.

**Software evidence:** N06b real target editor + N06 or S05 target-attainment result.

**EXAMPLE:** Fictional inverter target, July–September 2026: 2,046 actual versus 2,180 target = 93.9% attainment.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Quantify the gap and determine whether further account support or a product review is worthwhile.

**Qualification:** Targets are entered manually; they are not imported from a live CRM.

## 08 — Read the Installer Portfolio
**Main statement:** Prioritise the accounts that shape the activity of your tracked channel.

**YOU CAN:** Rank installers, see active-account counts, Top 5 concentration, contributions to change and monthly activity heatmaps.

**Software evidence:** N07b searchable ranking + N08 monthly heatmap; N07 KPI strip as a reserve.

**EXAMPLE:** July–September 2026: 22 active accounts and 34.2% of tracked activations concentrated in the Top 5.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Understand dependence on major accounts and where emerging or declining activity deserves attention.

**Qualification:** Shares cover this imported installer population, not the whole Irish market.

## 09 — Investigate an Installer in Detail
**Main statement:** Move from a portfolio signal to an evidence-backed account profile.

**YOU CAN:** Find any tracked installer or source alias; review its selected-period activity, last recorded activation, historical trend, momentum, ranking and source evidence.

**Software evidence:** N13 real installer profile and R03 historic-month chart, where readable.

**EXAMPLE:** Open DEMO Harbourlight Solar Ltd and compare 40 activations in April–June with 23 in July–September 2026.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Enter an account conversation with facts and useful questions, rather than assumptions.

**Qualification:** A decline cannot on its own identify the cause.

## 10 — Compare 2–6 Installers Side by Side
**Main statement:** Compare partner trends on a consistent scale.

**YOU CAN:** Select between two and six accounts, review absolute activity and, when eligible, compare indexed trends against a positive baseline.

**Software evidence:** Existing S07 three-installer comparison; keep selection names and chart legible.

**EXAMPLE:** Compare Silverfern, Cedarfield and Harbourlight over the same complete reporting months.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Distinguish a portfolio-wide pattern from an individual account's movement.

**Qualification:** Indexed growth only appears when every selected account has a complete positive first bucket; otherwise use absolute volumes.

## 11 — Spot Growth, Decline, Inactivity and Return
**Main statement:** Turn the portfolio into a practical queue of accounts worth reviewing.

**YOU CAN:** Filter rule-based insights for Declining, Growing, Inactive, Reactivated and data checks; tune minimum volume and percentage-change thresholds.

**Software evidence:** N09 genuine multi-signal account-priorities table; highlight a few real rows with PPT-native annotations.

**EXAMPLE:** The fictional July–September 2026 dataset contains two Declining, one Growing, one Inactive and one Reactivated signal.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Prioritise focused support and follow-up while directing incomplete cases to data validation first.

**Qualification:** Deterministic rules, not churn prediction, competitor identification or real-time alerts.

## 12 — Keep Coverage and Account Identity Reliable
**Main statement:** Reliable conclusions begin with reliable source history.

**YOU CAN:** Verify expected equipment coverage, distinguish recorded zeros from unknown months and manually review suggested duplicate installer names before combining them.

**Software evidence:** N10 category coverage plus the authentic pre-consolidation S08 alias suggestion.

**EXAMPLE:** A missing source/month record is not equivalent to a confirmed three-month pause.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Prevent false inactivity alarms and inaccurate partner rankings.

**Qualification:** The name-union action is reviewed by a person and source history remains auditable.

## 13 — Turn One Signal into a Commercial Follow-Up
**Main statement:** Use a verified movement to start the right conversation at the right account.

**YOU CAN:** Confirm comparable coverage, open the profile, quantify the change, review context and choose an outreach action.

**Software evidence:** Existing S09 High/Declining row, R03 historical evidence, native 40 → 23 (−42.5%).

**EXAMPLE:** DEMO Harbourlight Solar Ltd: April–June 2026: 40 activations; July–September: 23; difference −17.

**COMMERCIAL ACTION:** Validate → Contact partner → Ask about pipeline, pricing, stock, training and support → Agree next step → Review after the next complete import.

**Qualification:** Outreach, CRM ownership and intervention logging happen outside the application.

## 14 — Export Evidence and Protect Your Workspace
**Main statement:** Keep the analysis portable, reviewable and recoverable.

**YOU CAN:** Export the installer insights or other supported CSV summaries, download a full JSON backup, and restore a reviewed workspace backup.

**Software evidence:** N11 CSV export control and N12 Backup & guide (do not show fictitious demo mode to prove export).

**EXAMPLE:** The actual test CSV contains 23 fictional installer rows. The actual downloaded JSON backup contains the 675 equipment records, 23 consolidated installers and saved targets.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** Prepare structured evidence for offline review and protect work when changing browsers or computers.

**Qualification:** Browser storage can be session-only; use approved confidential storage for exported files. No automatic CRM synchronisation.

## 15 — Start in Minutes, Then Review Monthly
**Main statement:** A simple workflow makes the analysis repeatable.

**HOW TO START:** (1) Extract the official V2.1.0 ZIP; (2) open root `index.html` in a browser; (3) choose **Explore fictional demo** or import approved activation reports; (4) verify coverage and review the dashboards; (5) export a full backup.

**MONTHLY REVIEW LOOP:** New reports → Validate data → Inspect trends and account priorities → Contact partners → Record actions externally → Recheck the next complete month.

**Software evidence:** Native editable workflow and one official release hyperlink, no tiny screenshots.

**COMMERCIAL VALUE:** A practical repeatable habit, not another spreadsheet to read once.

**Qualification:** The built-in fictional demo differs from the **extended January 2023–September 2026 synthetic dataset** shown here, and disables export. All screenshots show illustrative synthetic data, not actual Huawei results.

## Shared presentation rule set

- **One purpose per slide**. Avoid reusing the same commercial example on unrelated slides.
- Each module slide must make “What you can do → Real UI proof → Business implication” clear **without live narration**.
- No slide needs more than two image crops; crop UI to the *source evidence*, not to empty browser margins.
- All copy/metrics and annotations are editable native PowerPoint text. Genuine UI remains unaltered PNG.
- Keep 16:9 white/charcoal/pale-grey/Huawei-inspired red, consistent title/footer and visible labels.
- State **Illustrative synthetic data — not actual Huawei results** on every slide showing fake values.
- Do not imply live source sync, automatic alerts, direct revenue, national market share or installer-to-model mapping.
- G1 copy is frozen for prototyping; any later text correction must be logged against a screenshot/source check rather than made silently.
