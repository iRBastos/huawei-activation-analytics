# Huawei Activation Analytics V2.1.0

An offline Huawei equipment and installer activation dashboard, focused on evidence-based Channel Management decisions.

## What's included
- Installer rankings, activity, account profiles, three-month momentum and comparisons of 2–6 installers.
- Explainable decline, inactivity, reactivation and growth signals with coverage checks and CSV export.
- Inverter, battery module and BMS analytics with targets, history and product catalogue.
- Refined V2.1 navigation and performance optimizations for larger installer datasets.
- Local XLSX/CSV imports, full JSON backups, fictional demo and a standalone offline application.
- Commercial overview, Channel Manager playbook, user guide, quick start and documentation audit in **English, Brazilian Portuguese and Simplified Chinese** (Markdown and HTML).

## Getting started
Download **Huawei_Activation_Analytics_V2.1.0.zip** from the Assets section, extract it and open **index.html**. No server, Node.js, Python or internet connection is needed to use the dashboard. Open **docs/commercial/index.html** for the multilingual manuals. Back up existing local data under **Data → Backup & guide** before replacing the project.

## Important boundaries
Activations are not revenue, sales, verified installation volumes, churn probabilities or competitor switching evidence. Installer-company figures and equipment-model figures are separate datasets. Activity signals are rule-based and depend on complete monthly data imports, not live notifications or CRM automation. No confidential user data is bundled. Real customer import/backup acceptance remains the owner's responsibility.
