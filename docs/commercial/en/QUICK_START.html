Commercial Quick Start — 10-minute account review

Goal: Find material changes in Huawei installer activation activity, qualify the evidence and choose an appropriate account action. This is a suggested operating process, not an automated real-time alert.

  1. Refresh the source. Import the latest monthly installer XLSX/CSV via Data → Installer imports. Confirm whether each month is Full or Partial coverage. Missing months are unknown, not zero.
  2. Select comparable periods. In Installers, use closed, like-for-like reporting months and note the latest reliable month.
  3. Screen signals. In Insights & priorities, start with the existing defaults: at least 5 activations and 20% change. Look at declines, inactivity, reactivation and growth.
  4. Validate. If the result says Data check, confirm missing data rather than concluding that an installer is declining.
  5. Investigate accounts. Use Installer profile and Compare 2–6 to inspect historical movement and source evidence.
  6. Act commercially. Contact the partner; check pipeline, pricing, stock, support, commissioning and training. Offer specific help based on confirmed causes.
  7. Follow up. Record owner, action and review date in an external CRM or action tracker. Reassess after the next complete monthly import and save a full JSON backup.

Important: Recorded activations are not sales, verified installations, market share, or proof of a competitor switch. The application does not send notifications or automate partner outreach.
