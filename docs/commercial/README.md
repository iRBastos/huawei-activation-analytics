**Boundaries:** Insights are rule-based and recalculate after manual data import. They are not real-time alerts or evidence of sales, revenue, market share or competitor switching. Contact SLAs and follow-ups are suggested commercial practices managed outside the app. Equipment-by-model and installer-by-company figures remain separate datasets. No real customer data or PPT is included.
